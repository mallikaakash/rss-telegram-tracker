Decoding in Tiles: A Static Megakernel for Llama 3.2 1B on an RTX 3050 | Tobias Rapp | Performance engineering for GPU and AI systems

A detailed, original performance engineering deep-dive on designing a static megakernel to eliminate kernel launch bubbles for batch-1 inference on consumer GPUs.
  • Breaks down runtime overheads for Llama 3.2 1B, showing PyTorch uses 787 kernel launches and llama.cpp uses 213 per token, introducing significant idle bubbles.
  • Contrasts the latency and bandwidth bottlenecks of megakernels on an 8 GB RTX 3050 versus datacenter H100/B200 GPUs.
  • Details the design of TileInfer, an ahead-of-time planned persistent kernel maximizing memory bandwidth saturation at batch size one.

Is it RL or Data? A Controlled Comparison of GRPO and Rejection-Sampling Fine-Tuning

A rigorous, pre-registered empirical study isolating whether GRPO's gains over rejection-sampling fine-tuning stem from RL dynamics or merely implicit prompt difficulty filtering.
  • Compares GRPO and rejection-sampling fine-tuning (RFT) under strictly matched compute budgets, prompts, and a 27-run hyperparameter sweep for RFT.
  • Demonstrates that GRPO beats RFT by 10.9 points on curated prompts, and curating prompts actually degrades RFT (-2.7 points), disproving the hypothesis that GRPO's edge is purely passive data filtering.
  • Shows that iterated RFT with policy resampling recovers only ~30% (3.2 points) of the performance delta.
