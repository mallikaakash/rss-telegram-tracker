[2608.26319] When Is Noise Response Universal? Tokenization as the Hidden Variable in Language Models

Uncovers the mechanical reason why neural models diverge under character noise but follow universal degradation curves under word-level noise.
  • Discovers that textual neural models degrade along an identical universal curve under word noise regardless of architecture, but diverge under character noise.
  • Identifies token re-segmentation as the hidden variable: character edits force tokenizers to reshape surrounding tokens, whereas word drops preserve token boundaries.
  • Shows eight disparate encoders collapse onto a universal robustness curve after a brief contrastive pretraining step.
