🔎 I tried to make language-model pretraining faster by compressing text. | Illia Zakharov

An insightful postmortem detailing empirical negative results from attempting to accelerate pretraining via text compression.
  • Compressing text yielded a 4x sequence shortening and 3.67x faster training, but failed due to lossy token representations
  • Reconstruction accuracy capped at 56.9%, leading to worse generation quality than standard BPE baselines
  • Downstream repair models and larger generator capacities failed to recover information lost during initial text compression

Under the Hood of MLX: How a Model Runs on a Mac | TensorFoundry

A useful technical deep dive into MLX internals, explaining graph construction, lazy evaluation, and kernel fusion on Apple Silicon.
  • MLX defers execution by default, building an operation graph until mx.eval is explicitly invoked
  • mx.compile traces the operation graph to fuse chains of operations into unified GPU kernels
  • Kernel fusion reduces GPU dispatch overhead and command buffer submission frequency
