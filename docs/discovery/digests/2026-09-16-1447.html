ReCo: Reweighting GRPO Against Distributional Concentration | alphaXiv

Identifies an important failure mode in GRPO reasoning models (degraded Pass@k at high k) and presents an intuitive reweighting fix.
  • Discovers that GRPO degrades reasoning coverage (Pass@k for large k) due to high-probability responses dominating the group gradient and importance ratios reinforcing saturated tokens.
  • Proposes ReCo, which normalizes response gradients by rollout frequency and switches to a variance-based token importance ratio at non-saturated decision points.
  • Demonstrates improved Pass@k scaling on mathematical reasoning benchmarks across Qwen2.5-Math and Llama-3.1 models.
