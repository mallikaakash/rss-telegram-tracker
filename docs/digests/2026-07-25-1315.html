European Union grants US request to restrict satellite images of Iran War region

  • The European Union has begun delaying the release of satellite images showing shipping lanes near the Strait of Hormuz by 24 hours. The move comes in response to an earlier US government request and coincides with the Trump administration ramping up the war with Iran once again. The unusual change in the free and open data policy for Europe’s Copernicus Earth observation program was revealed by Space News , which reviewed a copy of the decision made by the Council of the EU on July 13.
