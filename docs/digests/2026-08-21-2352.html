Cobalt is an open-source application platform for Kobo e-readers: a launcher, a signed App Store, a Rust SDK, and a runtime that keeps every app in its own unprivileged process. Install it once over USB. Every app after that installs, updates and removes on the reader itself, over Wi-Fi.
