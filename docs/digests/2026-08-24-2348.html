MetaRoCE: A New RDMA Transport Built for AI-Scale Ethernet

  • Training and serving frontier AI models depends on fast, reliable networks that move data between GPUs without wasting compute cycles. To meet this challenge at scale, Meta designed MetaRoCE – a clean-sheet RDMA transport protocol purpose-built for AI workloads on commodity Ethernet. We’re releasing the MetaRoCE specification, a reference software implementation and a compliance test suite through the Open Compute Project (OCP) to enable the broader industry to adopt, implement, and build on it.
