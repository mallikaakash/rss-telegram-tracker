Vulcan failure caused by inadequate fuel supply to engines, ULA says

  • United Launch Alliance said Wednesday that the failure of its Vulcan rocket during a launch on Sunday was caused by inadequate fuel supply. "ULA has completed an initial integrated review of flight data from the Leo 1 launch anomaly that occurred last Sunday," the company said in a statement . "While work remains to determine cause, preliminary indications are that the low booster thrust was the result of inadequate fuel supply from the vehicle to the BE-4 engines, not from the performance of the engines." The new information released by ULA confirms reporting from Ars Technica on Tuesday that the rocket's failure was not due to the performance of the vehicle's main engines, which are provided by Blue Origin.
