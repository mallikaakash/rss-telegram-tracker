Dogfooding at scale: migrating cdnjs to Cloudflare’s Developer Platform

  • As of June 23, 2026, cdnjs, one of the Internet's busiest open-source CDNs, is running exclusively on Cloudflare’s Developer Platform . Along the way, cdnjs surfaced limits in the platform, and the platform grew to meet them. cdnjs is a free, open-source content delivery network for JavaScript and CSS libraries. Instead of using a bundler or self-hosting jQuery, Bootstrap, or Lodash, you drop a <script> tag pointing to cdnjs.cloudflare.com and the library loads from Cloudflare's edge, instantly, anywhere in the world, with no signup, no API keys, and no rate limits.
