Backblaze explains why it's sharing customer information with advertisers

  • Some customers have raised concerns about Backblaze's privacy policy taking effect next month, which says the backup and cloud storage company may sell certain personal information to advertisers. Backblaze’s current privacy policy says that in addition to the personal information that users may provide, such as their email address when creating an account, it may also “automatically” collect information, such as “your software, the operating system you use when accessing our service, your Internet Protocol address, and the date and time of each request you make to Backblaze.” The current policy adds: “Under no circumstances do we rent, trade, or share your address or e-mail address with any other company for their marketing purposes without your consent.” It also makes no mention of advertisers.

The FCC has authorized SpaceX to deploy 1 million satellites

  • In an extraordinary decision, the Federal Communications Commission on Wednesday granted SpaceX conditional authorization to deploy a constellation of up to 1 million Starmind satellites designed to serve as orbital data centers. If fully deployed, this constellation would increase the number of satellites in orbit by roughly 50-fold. "The United States is moving decisively to ensure that this breakthrough technology creates opportunity, drives investment, and delivers lasting benefits for the American people," FCC Chairman Brendan Carr said in a statement on Wednesday.

  • After backlash from sellers frustrated by Etsy’s new AI policies, which appeared to allow the company to train AI on user artwork, Etsy has now clarified its stance.
