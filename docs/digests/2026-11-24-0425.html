Many languages, one common neural code

  • Languages take on thousands of forms. Some are tonal, like Vietnamese, where shifts in pitch convey distinct meanings. English relies on precise word orders, but this matters less for Russian, where words can be swapped without changing the intended message. Despite so much variety in the linguistic kingdom, an MIT study has found that the human brain may process all sorts of languages in a universal way.
