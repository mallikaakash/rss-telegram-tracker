Simulating student learning for better AI tutoring

  • At a glance StudentSim is a research system for evaluating and improving AI tutors before they are tested with real learners; it is not a product, is not deployed in classrooms, and is not intended to replace teachers AI tutoring tools need feedback from students to improve, but collecting that feedback through human studies is slow, costly, and difficult to scale StudentSim trains personalized AI student simulators from publicly available, de-identified learner records, with one simulator adapted to each student The work evaluates simulators along two education-grounded dimensions: whether they respond like a specific student, and whether they update after tutor guidance Across chess, second-language English writing, and mathematics, StudentSim outperforms leading baselines and provides a useful feedback signal for training an AI tutor In research settings, teams are exploring how AI-powered tutoring systems can help explain a math problem, suggest an edit to a writing assignment, or guide a learner through a chess position.
