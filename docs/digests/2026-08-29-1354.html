Samsung's Processing-in-Memory (PIM)

Excellent hardware performance deep-dive on Processing-in-Memory (PIM) architecture for accelerating low-precision ML inference.
  • Places MAC units directly at each of the 16 DRAM banks to achieve 614 GB/s internal bandwidth compared to 76.8 GB/s external bus limits.
  • Sustains 8 INT8/FP8 MAC ops per cycle per PIM block, reaching 2.4 INT8 TOPS per package (9.6 TOPS across 8 chips).
  • Maintains compatibility with standard LPDDR5X protocols by mapping compute controls and multi-bank mode to designated row addresses.
