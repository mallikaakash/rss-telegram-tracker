Choosing QuePaxa, an unusual consensus algorithm

  • Cloudflare recently announced Meerkat , an experimental distributed consensus service designed to manage our internal global control plane. It is powered by a new consensus algorithm published by researchers Tennage & Băsescu et al. called QuePaxa (2023). Google DeepMind just mathematically verified key parts of Signal

  • Google DeepMind researchers used agentic AI to mathematically prove, or “formally verify,” a number of the Signal protocol’s key cryptographic guarantees—a landmark development for the widely used cryptography and AI itself. Conducted as a sort of proof-of-concept project in just four weeks, the work is significant in two ways. It serves as further independent validation of the cryptography that protects massive quantities of the world's communications on platforms like the Signal app , Google Messages, and WhatsApp.
