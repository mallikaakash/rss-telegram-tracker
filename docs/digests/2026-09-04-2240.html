The Two Abstractions of System Design: Hide or Reduce

Directly strengthens distributed systems engineering fundamentals by formalizing the difference between code modularity and state-machine reduction.
  • Distinguishes 'modularity abstraction' (encapsulating code, hiding internals and interleavings) from 'modeling abstraction' (reducing behaviors to minimal skeletons to verify invariants).
  • Shows how modularity accidentally hides concurrency and efficiency opportunities, whereas modeling abstraction exposes safe interleavings to maximize concurrency.
  • Explains why software engineers struggle with formal verification tools like TLA+ when trying to hide complexity instead of reducing state spaces.
