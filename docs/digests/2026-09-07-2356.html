The Dataflow Model Revisited

Essential distributed systems deep-dive where the original authors re-evaluate the Dataflow model on its VLDB Test of Time award, directly addressing stream/batch infra architecture.
  • Retains core foundations: event-time primacy, unviability of waiting for data completeness, and strong consistency aged well.
  • Concedes that explicit windowing/triggering was an over-engineered UX failure; the stream-table duality and SQL-based incremental view maintenance (IVM) with freshness contracts proved superior.
  • Highlights how completeness evolved into two paradigms: watermarks (visible streams) and snapshot-consistent refresh (materialized views), with the latter dominating mainstream usage.
