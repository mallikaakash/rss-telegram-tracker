Cache-to-Cache: Direct Semantic Communication Between Large Language Models

Proposes a novel inter-LLM communication architecture fusing KV-caches directly instead of decoding to tokens, advancing multi-model inference efficiency.
  • Cache-to-Cache (C2C) uses a neural network projection and learnable gating to fuse source and target KV-caches directly across layers.
  • Eliminates token-by-token intermediate text generation latency, yielding an average 2.5x speedup.
  • Delivers 6.4-14.2% accuracy gains over single models and 3.1-5.4% over standard text-based inter-model communication.
