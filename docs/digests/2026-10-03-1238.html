Limits of Confidence in Diffusion

Directly impacts core ML modeling theory by revealing mathematical constraints in discrete diffusion sequence and token generation.
  • Proves discrete diffusion sampling only matches training distributions when simultaneously written token positions are conditionally independent given fixed tokens.
  • Demonstrates that products of independent per-position distributions fundamentally fail to capture dependent token groups in sequence domains like text or audio.
  • Provides critical theoretical bounds for evaluating non-autoregressive and discrete diffusion alternatives to standard autoregressive LLMs.
