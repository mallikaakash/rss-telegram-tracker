In reversal, American Cancer Society urges routine prostate cancer screening

  • In a significant update to its guidelines for prostate cancer screening, the American Cancer Society is now recommending that men get routinely screened for prostate cancer with a PSA test. Men at average risk should get a PSA test every two years from age 50 to 69, according to the new guidelines released Thursday. Men considered at high risk due to race and family history should start testing earlier, at 45, and most are recommended to get tested every year.
