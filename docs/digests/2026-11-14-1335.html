FAA has lifted its third-ever nationwide ground stop

  • The Federal Administration lifted its third-ever nationwide ground stop after several hours Friday evening. Domestic flights were cleared to begin taking off at 8:35 pm EST. FAA Transportation Secretary Sean Duffy cautioned that, barring massive system updates, there will be "more of these" ahead. "Today's nationwide ground stop happened because our air traffic control system is operating on DECADES-OLD analog equipment that simply cannot compete with the demands of the 21st century," Duffy posted on X .

datasette-leaflet 0.3

  • Release: datasette-leaflet 0.3 OpenStreetMap have a very reasonable policy that hits to their hosted tile server should send a referrer header . Datasette table pages used the Referrer-Policy: no-referrer HTTP header, causing plugins that loaded tiles from OpenStreetMap to violate that policy. datasette-leaflet is the plugin that loads a bundled Leaflet so other Datasette plugins can depend on it.
