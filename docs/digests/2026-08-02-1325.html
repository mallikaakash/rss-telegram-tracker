Running Kimi K3 on MI355X at Better Performance per Dollar Than B300

Crucial ML infrastructure benchmark comparing AMD MI355X against NVIDIA B200/B300 GPUs for serving the 2.8T parameter Kimi K3 model under memory and interconnect constraints.
  • Kimi K3 (2.8T parameters) requires >1.5TB VRAM before KV cache, forcing multi-node TP16 on 192GB B200s versus single-node TP8 on 288GB MI355X or B300 nodes.
  • AMD MI355X achieves 952 tok/s/node aggregate throughput, outperforming B200 TP16 by 3.8x and beating B300 on performance per dollar (48 vs 33 tok/s/$).
  • Multi-node TP16 decode on B200s suffers heavy latency penalties due to cross-node RoCE v2 all-reduce on the decode critical path.
