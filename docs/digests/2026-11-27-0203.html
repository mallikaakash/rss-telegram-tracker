Ford unveils the GT41 as it hopes to win Le Mans again

  • Sixty years ago, after two failed attempts, Ford won the 24 Hours of Le Mans with its GT40 race car. Draining peatlands is responsible for a few percent of our global warming

  • Much of the focus for human-caused climate change is on fossil fuels, which are creating most of our problems. But land use change is also a large component of the story, accounting for about one-third of total historical CO 2 emissions. Deforestation plays a major role here, but practices like draining wetlands also factor in. Exactly how much of our history of eliminating wetlands has contributed to warming is challenging to figure out.
