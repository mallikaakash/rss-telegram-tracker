4 real? Tracking exploitation and patching of eMudhra’s weak keys

Top-tier reliability and security post-mortem from Cloudflare detailing widespread CA key failures and custom mitigation in Pingora.
  • eMudhra CA generated absurdly weak private keys (single/low digits like 4, 56, 115) allowing instantaneous offline key recovery by anyone.
  • Cloudflare leveraged Rust-based Pingora origin proxies to detect and block weak handshakes, logging ~22,000 exploitation attempts in one week.
  • Employed AI-assisted codebase scanning to triage and patch internal systems across the organization within hours.
