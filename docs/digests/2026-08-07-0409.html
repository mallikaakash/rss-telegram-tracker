AMD acquires Taalas to boost inference performance by etching models in silicon

Covers a novel hardware paradigm of etching model weights into silicon (MSICs) to bypass HBM bottlenecks for high-throughput inference.
  • AMD acquired Toronto startup Taalas, which bakes fixed model weights directly into silicon masks rather than dynamic HBM.
  • Early TSMC 6nm test chips served Llama 3.1 8B at 16,960 tokens/sec, drastically outperforming standard GPUs.
  • Architecture combines mask-ROM for baked parameters with SRAM for dynamic KV caches and fine-tuning adapters.
