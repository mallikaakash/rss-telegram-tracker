Is the Industrial Revolution a good precedent for explosive growth today?

Provides a quantitative economic analysis challenging claims that AI will trigger explosive 10x GDP growth based on Industrial Revolution precedents.
  • Pre-Industrial Revolution annual GDP growth frequently spiked over 10x its compound average due to high historical baseline volatility.
  • Modern growth is significantly less volatile, making a 10x acceleration today fundamentally different from historical shifts.
  • An Industrial Revolution-scale shift in modern frontier economies would yield ~2.8% annual growth, far below 20%+ hypergrowth claims.
