Postgres with QUIC

A high-impact backend networking deep-dive demonstrating how QUIC stream multiplexing solves synchronous client-side connection pooling limits in Postgres.
  • Replaced TCP connection pooling with QUIC stream multiplexing in PgCat, multiplexing hundreds of virtual streams over few UDP associations.
  • Under heavy WAN load, QUIC sustained 9,718 QPS at 246ms latency, whereas TCP collapsed with 40,000 backlogged queries at 7,088ms latency.
  • Dissects Postgres Protocol 3.0's synchronous 1-connection-per-query constraint and why client-side poolers choke across WAN latencies.
