The Thin Orchestrator: How We Made Our AI Agents More Reliable by Giving the Router Less to Do

Meta engineering case study on reducing agent hallucinations by shrinking orchestrator context and isolating specialized subagents.
  • Replaced a monolithic domain-aware agent with a 1,300-line deterministic router and 14 isolated domain specialist subagents.
  • Showed that loading an orchestrator with cross-stage schema context caused concept cross-contamination and dropped task accuracy to 10-20%.
  • Validated against empirical research showing multi-agent task isolation maintains 65.3% accuracy where single agents degrade to 16.6%.
